# Primary market

**[Minting](mint.md) and [redeeming](burn.md) directly with the [issuer](issuer.md).** `[industry]`

## Who takes part

Onboarded, KYC'd institutions with an account at the issuer, such as exchanges, market makers and the partner itself. Everyone else gets tokens on the [secondary market](secondary-market.md). `[industry]`

## Why it matters

It is the only place where tokens are created or destroyed, so it is where the issuer controls supply. `[industry]`
