# Attestation

**An independent accounting firm's report confirming that the [reserves](reserves.md) existed and matched the token supply on a given date.** `[industry]`

## Why it matters

The issuer's own numbers aren't enough. Holders, partners and regulators trust a third party's check. `[industry]`

## Attestation vs. audit

An attestation checks the reserves on one date. An audit examines the issuer's financial statements across a whole period. The GENIUS Act requires a monthly public report of reserve composition, examined by an accounting firm. `[industry]`
