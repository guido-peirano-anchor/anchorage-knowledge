# Stablecoin

**A token on a blockchain designed to hold a steady price, usually $1.** `[industry]`

## Why it matters

A stablecoin gives you a dollar that moves at blockchain speed, around the clock, and that on-chain applications can use. Other crypto assets move in price too much to work as money. `[industry]`

## Kinds

- **[Fiat-backed](fiat-backed-stablecoin.md)**: backed by cash and government debt held off chain. This is the kind Anchorage issues.
- **Crypto-collateralized**: backed by more crypto than the tokens are worth, locked on chain.
- **Algorithmic or synthetic**: the price is held by a mechanism or a trading strategy rather than by reserves.
