# Secondary market

**Buying, selling and moving tokens that already exist, without the [issuer](issuer.md).** `[industry]`

## Why it matters

The issuer doesn't control this market, but it is where the $1 peg is visible. A price below $1 here signals doubt about redemption in the [primary market](primary-market.md). `[industry]`

## At Anchorage

Internally, "secondary market" also covers **bridging**: moving an existing coin from one blockchain to another. `[atlas]`
