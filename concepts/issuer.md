# Issuer

**The legal entity that creates and redeems a stablecoin. It owns the promise to pay $1 per token and controls who may mint and burn.** `[industry]`

## Issuer vs. brand

The company whose name is on the coin is not always the issuer. A brand can partner with a regulated issuer, which then holds the [reserves](reserves.md) and runs [minting](mint.md) and [burning](burn.md) for the brand. `[industry]`

## At Anchorage

- Anchorage Digital Bank, a federally chartered bank, acts as issuer and operator for partner coins. For example, the atlas describes USDtb (Ethena) as issued by Anchorage Digital Bank. `[atlas]`
- The production issuer organizations are ADB Stablecoin Issuer, ADB-NA USAT Issuer and OSL Group. `[atlas]`
